Hi, I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I am an applied microeconomist specializing in environmental, energy economics, and public economics, with research interests in environmental policy, electricity markets, digital infrastructure, and local economic impacts.

My research uses causal inference, geospatial methods, and large-scale data to study how policy, infrastructure, and technological change affect markets, communities, and the environment.
